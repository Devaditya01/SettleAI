# Settle — visual direction

Midnight uses charcoal, ivory, and muted sage. Pearl uses warm ivory and soft green. The landing pages and dashboard share these palettes, with an edition switch in the dashboard.

The landing pages retain their original asymmetric composition, large italic headlines, money illustrations, colours, and animations. Supporting copy is shortened without changing the layout. The dashboard retains its separate visual refinement.

## Content and layout

The original landing-page sections and navigation are preserved. Both editions include a Terms navigation link and a matching Terms & Conditions page. The landing-page footer has been removed so the final call to action ends the page cleanly.

The dashboard has shorter page titles, larger transaction text, simpler navigation, and fewer introductory messages. Settlement explanations, exceptions, fees, timelines, and source records remain available. On small screens, the summary becomes a vertical list and decorative dashboard artwork is omitted to keep the data accessible.

## Startup product story

A platform section extends both landing pages without changing the original layout or theme. It explains four working capabilities, the payment-data categories the product is designed to connect, and the standard planned for production controls. Claims about live integrations and customer outcomes are deliberately excluded until they can be verified.

## Payment illustration

The hero follows the synthetic TXN-1041 fixture: INR 8,499.00 captured, INR 169.98 in fees, and INR 8,329.02 credited after a successful retry. The three controls select each stage. An automatic cycle advances every 4.5 seconds while the hero is visible. The dashboard contains the complete attempt history.

Reduced-motion preferences stop ambient animation and automatic cycling. The footer motion toggle pauses both. Cycling also pauses while the hero is off-screen or the document is hidden.

## Files

Open index.html or pearl.html. Both launch dashboard/index.html with the selected theme. Landing styles live in assets/css/ and scripts in assets/js/. Dashboard styles and scripts live in dashboard/css/ and dashboard/js/. Keep this folder structure when sharing.

All visuals use HTML, CSS, and inline SVG. The demo works offline, apart from external links. Data is synthetic and answers use local rules; there are no live payments, database, authentication, or LLM calls.
